Chiyun Noh

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the RPM Robotics lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Ayoung Kim.

I work on SLAM, sensor fusion, and robot perception with a focus on radar, LiDAR, and quadrupedal robot systems. My goal is to develop robust perception and navigation algorithms for challenging real-world environments.
