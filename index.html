Radar-Inertial Odometry · Sensor Fusion

Chiyun Noh

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the RPM Robotics lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Ayoung Kim.

My research interests include Radar-Inertial Odometry and Sensor Fusion. I am particularly interested in leveraging the estimated local gravity to refine the rotation matrix.

Publications

GaRLIO: Gravity enhanced Radar-LiDAR-Inertial Odometry

Chiyun Noh, Wooseong Yang, Minwoo Jung, Sangwoo Jung, Ayoung Kim

IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025

arXiv Code

GaRLIO, the proposed gravity-enhanced Radar-LiDAR-Inertial Odometry, can robustly predict gravity to reduce vertical drift while simultaneously enhancing state estimation performance using pointwise velocity measurements.

HeRCULES: Heterogeneous Radar Dataset in Complex Urban Environment for Multi-session Radar SLAM

Hanjun Kim, Minwoo Jung, Chiyun Noh, Sangwoo Jung, Hyunho Song, Wooseong Yang, Hyesu Jang, Ayoung Kim

IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025

arXiv Dataset site

HeRCULES introduces the first public dataset with 4D radar, scanning radar, and FMCW LiDAR, enabling radar-LiDAR fusion SLAM and cross-sensor place recognition across diverse urban conditions.
